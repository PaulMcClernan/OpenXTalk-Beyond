# Android card: a click on the "App Bundle" box (patch -1) stores the
# "android,app bundle" setting, which the Android builder reads, instead of
# being taken for an architecture (its second word, "Bundle", is none). The
# architectures' boxes do what they did.
file: Toolset/palettes/standalone settings/revstandalonesettings.8.rev
object: group "Android Targets" of card "Android"
replace:
|   put the short name of the target into tTarget|
|   updateAndroidArch word 2 of tTarget, the hilite of the target|
with:
|   put the short name of the target into tTarget|
|   -- OXT-Beyond: "App Bundle" is no architecture: it asks the build for|
|   -- an Android App Bundle (.aab) beside the package|
|   if tTarget is "App Bundle" then|
|      storeSetting "app bundle", the hilite of the target|
|      exit mouseUp|
|   end if|
|   updateAndroidArch word 2 of tTarget, the hilite of the target|
