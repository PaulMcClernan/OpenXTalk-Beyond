# The macOS Native App Tools extension's sample, MacOS-Native-Tools-Tester:
# its buttons brought the IDE back to the front with the AppleScript
# 'tell application "LiveCode" to activate', which on a Mac without
# LiveCode cannot find the application and, with it, brings LiveCode to
# the front instead of the IDE. They name OXT-Beyond, the IDE's application
# (CFBundleName in tools/oxt/package.py).
# This is the AppHide & AppUnhide button (with the line before, as the
# other button has the same line).
# Text lines are between bars, so that trailing blanks survive editors.
file: Extensions/org.openxtalk.library.macosnativeapptools/samples/MacOS-Native-Tools-Tester.oxtstack
object: button "AppHide & AppUnhide" of card 1
replace:
|   AppUnhide -- the windowID of this stack|
|   do "tell application "& quote & "LiveCode" & quote & " to activate" as AppleScript|
with:
|   AppUnhide -- the windowID of this stack|
|   do "tell application "& quote & "OXT-Beyond" & quote & " to activate" as AppleScript|
