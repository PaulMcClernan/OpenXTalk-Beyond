Sorting a list

A list in xTalk is just text: items separated by commas, or lines. sort puts them in order, in place. Text is sorted alphabetically unless you say otherwise:

sort tList on its own sorts lines, so sort lines of tList and sort tList are the same.

Capitals and accents

Text sorts by the order of the characters in Unicode, which puts every capital before every small letter when caseSensitive is true, and puts accented letters after z. Ordinary sorting ignores case.
