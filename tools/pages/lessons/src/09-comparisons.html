Comparing text

Text is compared in alphabetical order, and xTalk does not mind capital letters unless you ask it to, with the property caseSensitive.

A property like caseSensitive that you set in a handler goes back to its default when the handler ends, so it cannot surprise the rest of your program.

And, or, not

Combine conditions with and (both must be true), or (at least one) and not (the opposite). Brackets make your meaning plain.
