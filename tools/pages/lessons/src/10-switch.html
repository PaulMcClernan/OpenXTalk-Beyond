One value, many cases

When you compare one value against many possibilities, switch is tidier than a long chain of else if. Each case gives a value; xTalk runs the lines of the first one that matches, until a break. default runs when nothing matched.

Several values, one answer

Cases with no lines between them share the lines that follow. Matching text ignores capitals, as every text comparison does.

Do not forget break

Without break, xTalk carries on into the next case's lines, whether it matches or not. Occasionally that is what you want; usually it is a bug.

Cases that are conditions

A switch with nothing after it takes a condition in each case, and runs the first that is true. It reads well for ranges.

Try it yourself

Write a switch that turns a month's number (1 to 12) into its season in the northern hemisphere: 12, 1 and 2 are winter, 3 to 5 spring, 6 to 8 summer, and 9 to 11 autumn. Try it with month 4.
