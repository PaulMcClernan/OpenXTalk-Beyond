Wildcard patterns

The pattern must match the whole line. Put * on both sides to find lines that contain something anywhere:

into puts the result in another variable and leaves the list as it was, so you can filter the same list several ways. Square brackets match one of a set of characters: [aeiou]* keeps lines that start with a vowel.

Items, and empty lines

Say items of to filter a comma-separated list. A very common job is dropping the blank lines from text: filter it without empty.

Filtering with regular expressions

For patterns a wildcard cannot say, filter with a regular expression. Unlike a wildcard, a regular expression matches anywhere in the line unless you anchor it with ^ (the start) or $ (the end). The lesson Regular expressions teaches the language of these patterns.
