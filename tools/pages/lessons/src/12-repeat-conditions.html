Repeat while

Sometimes you do not know how many times to loop, only when to stop. repeat while checks its condition before every turn, and stops as soon as it is false.

Repeat until

repeat until is the other way round: it loops until its condition becomes true. How many years does money take to double at 7% a year?

Leaving a loop: exit repeat

A plain repeat (or repeat forever) never stops by itself. exit repeat leaves the loop at once, from anywhere inside it.

Skipping a turn: next repeat

next repeat skips the rest of this turn and goes straight on to the next one.

A famous loop

Start with any whole number. If it is even, halve it; if it is odd, triple it and add one. Mathematicians believe you always reach 1, but nobody has proved it (it is the Collatz conjecture).

A loop whose condition never changes runs for ever, and the program seems to freeze. The Academy's playground stops a loop after ten seconds. In the IDE, stop a running script with Ctrl+. (Cmd+. on a Mac).
