Put into a chunk

Chunks are not only for reading. You can put text into a chunk to replace it, or before or after it to add to it, and xTalk changes just that piece of the variable.

Lists grow to fit

Put something into an item or line that does not exist yet, and xTalk adds the separators needed to reach it.

Delete a chunk

delete removes a chunk, and the separator with it, so a list stays a tidy list.

Replace every copy

replace changes every copy of one string into another, all through a variable or a field.

replace is the quickest way to change one separator into another: replace comma with return in tList turns a list of items into a list of lines.

Capitals

toUpper and toLower give a copy of a string in capitals or in small letters. They know the accented letters of other languages too, though a letter with no single capital, like the German ß, stays as it is.

Try it yourself

Take the quick brown fox and give every word a capital first letter, so it reads The Quick Brown Fox.
