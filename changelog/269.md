- Fields: in a right-to-left paragraph, the text after a closed embedding or isolate goes back to the paragraph's direction level, as the Unicode bidirectional algorithm (UAX #9, rule X1) and ICU have it, where it took the level of left-to-right text (stam66, openxtalk.org p=25479) (#269)
- Fields: a "$", "%" or "#" goes with the number next to it and nothing more (rule W5): a Hebrew or Arabic word between "$100" and "$200" is no longer laid out backwards as part of the numbers, and "50%" in right-to-left text no longer reads "%50" (#269)
- Fields: a ZWNJ, ZWJ or soft hyphen in a word, in text of the other direction, no longer splits the word into pieces laid out in the wrong order; an empty embedding, or one with only such a character in it, no longer changes the text around it; and the text either side of an isolate is resolved as one, as ICU does (UAX #9 section 5.2 and rule X10) (#269)
- Fields: in the cases above the runs of the `styledText` fall where the new direction levels change; the text, its styles, the `htmlText` and the `rtfText` are as before, and in a field whose textDirection is "ltr" or "auto", text with no right-to-left letters, Arabic digits or right-to-left marks lays out as before (#269)
