- HyperCard stacks: in a stack whose `hyperTalkCompatible` is true (File > Import Stack sets it), grouped text, HyperCard's hypertext, is drawn as HyperCard drew it: as the text it is, in its own colour, and underlined only after `show groups`, which such a stack starts without, where it was drawn in the link colour and underlined. A stack with a `linkColor`, `linkHiliteColor`, `linkVisitedColor` or `underlineLinks` of its own draws its link text with them (#260)
- HyperCard stacks: in such a stack, `the seconds`, and a date converted to or from seconds, count from midnight on 1 January 1904 in local time, as HyperCard's did, where they counted from 1970 in universal time: seconds a stack kept from HyperCard, or wrote in its scripts, are the dates they were, and a date's seconds with days of 86400 more are the days after it in any time zone, where across the end of daylight saving time they were an hour short. Seconds such a stack stored with 0.2.7-rc.6.3 to rc.6.5 read as dates 66 years earlier (#260)
