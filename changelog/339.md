- Mobile Web library (com.livecode.library.mobileweb), for stacks built for HTML5 on phones: one line, mwStart, makes a resizable stack fill the phone's screen (again when it turns) and scales a stack too big for it; swipe, pinch and longPress go to the control under the finger as mouse messages do; orientationChanged, keyboardActivated and keyboardDeactivated as on iOS and Android; finger scrolling and a buzz; opt-in, and on a desktop it does nothing (#339)
- Phone Starter: a stack to copy for phones, in portrait and landscape, made with the Mobile Web library, in the Stack Player and Home's gallery (#339)
