- HyperCard import: rule T46 puts HyperCard's windows where HyperCard put them. HyperCard gave a window's location as its top left: on the screen for the card window ("set the loc of card window to 0,20"), and from the card window's top left for every other window (the message box, a palette, a picture window, the Promptoid: "show msg at 15,300", "set the rect of window pictureWindow to 0,0,342,342"), while this engine gives a stack's location as its centre on the screen. A window's loc, rect, topLeft, bottomRight, top, left, bottom and right, read or set, and "show ... at", now go to the HyperCard Library, which places the window as HyperCard did, and the palette command's location is taken from the card window's top left too. "show card window", "hide card window" and "show card window at 0,0", which did nothing or stopped with an error, show, hide and place the stack's window. The message box's properties without "the" ("if visible of msg") and HyperCard's own windows named in quotes ("the visible of window "Scroll"") or shown and hidden ("show variable watcher") no longer stop the script (#240)
- HyperCard import: rule T46 also changes scripts that compile. Besides the places above, a window that is not open no longer stops the handler where its place is set or read: setting it leaves "No such window" in the result and reading it gives empty, as for HyperCard's own windows, which are not here. "show msg at h,v" moves the IDE's Message Box, and "window id 3" and "window 2" are left as they were (#240)
