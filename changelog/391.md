- export audioClip: `export audioClip "x" to file "x.wav"` writes a clip back out as a sound file with its header, WAV, AIFF or AU (`as wave`, `as aiff`, `as ulaw`, or by the file's extension), and `to` a variable puts a WAV file's bytes there, so the sounds the HyperCard importer makes, and any imported sound, can be got out of a stack again; `export videoClip` writes the file a video clip was imported from.
