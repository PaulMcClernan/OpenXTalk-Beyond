- HyperCard import: doMenu does HyperCard's File, Edit, Go, Objects, Style, Font and Paint menu items by their names (New Card, Paste Card, Bring Closer, Bold, a font, a size, Button Info…, Select All and Flip of the card's picture…), and takes a menu's name and "without dialog" (#231)
- Engine: in a stack whose hyperTalkCompatible is true, doMenu reaches the stack's doMenu handlers while messages are locked, as in HyperCard, and they run with messages still locked (#231)
- HyperCard import: rule T37 compiles more HyperTalk (words HyperCard took as their own text, a comma that ends a command's arguments, counts without "the", "the number of marked card i", a card "in this bg", "go cd" and "mark card" alone, "answer info", "put header into", and if...then...else forms that began an else block in HyperCard); in a corpus of 2,202 stacks, 1,501 more handlers compile and none fewer (#231)
- HyperCard import: rule T38 compiles "exit" and "pass" followed by the name of a handler other than the one they are in (they exit or pass the handler they are in), and "global" with names in quotes or nothing between two commas (#231)
- HyperCard import: a handler that does not compile keeps every line that does (rule T39). Each line it cannot compile is left out as a comment, and the handler runs past it. Where the line is a return, exit or pass, or is inside a loop that might not end without it, the handler stops there with an error instead. Before, the whole handler was left out. The import summary and the new revHyperCardLeftOutLines() list these lines. In the corpus of 2,202 stacks, handlers left out fall from 922 to 175: 734 are kept with lines left out of them, 13 more compile whole, and none are newly left out (#231)
- Engine: in a stack whose hyperTalkCompatible is true, lockRecent no longer keeps its cards out of the recent cards, so go back goes back through every card visited, as in HyperCard, whose lockRecent only kept cards out of its Recent dialog (#231)
