- Mobile Web library: each finger's touchStart, touchMove, touchEnd and touchRelease; the location, heading, acceleration and rotation-rate sensors (mwStartSensor); a photo from the camera or the library (mwPickPhoto), the share sheet (mwShare), the screen kept on (mwKeepAwake) and the browser's bars hidden (mwFullScreen); each does nothing on a desktop, and the Phone Starter has a Phone card that uses them (#348)
- Stacks for Phones: a guide to making a stack for phones with the Mobile Web library, in the Dictionary's guides (#348)
- Standalones: "Search for required inclusions" finds the Mobile Web library in a stack that calls it; every other library's inclusions are as before (#348)
- Stack Player: on a phone a stack has the whole screen, filled by a stack made for phones or fitted to it, with a button back to the library; elsewhere a stack is shown as before (#348)
- HTML5 standalones and the Stack Player: when the engine stops in a browser (out of the stack an iPhone or an iPad gives a page), WebAssembly is turned off (an iPhone's Lockdown Mode), the browser is older than the engine needs (before Safari 15 and iOS 15) or the engine's file is damaged, a note says which instead of a blank page (#348)
- Mobile Web library: mwPickPhoto, mwShare and mwFullScreen called other than from a tap say "not from a tap" where the browser can tell, instead of waiting for a photo that cannot come (#348)
