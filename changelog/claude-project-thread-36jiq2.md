- Stack Player: Wobble Forts, a physics game for phones on Box2Dxt: pull back and fling the round Bloops at the Grumps' wobbly forts of wood, glass, stone, metal and TNT, with a tap trick for four of the five Bloops; fifteen forts in three lands with a boss at the end of each, stars and best scores kept, Kenney's CC0 pictures and sounds; it fits a phone upright or turned, the keys alone play it on a computer, and it is in Home's gallery too
- Mobile Web deploy (tools/ci/mobile-web-deploy.livecodescript): OXT_MW_MODULES adds extension modules to a standalone, so a check can deploy a stack that uses one; without it nothing changes
- Web site: Wobble Forts has a page of its own in the web stacks gallery, with the big ones: on a computer it plays first on the page in the frame of a phone on its side, keys and mouse at once, and on a phone a tap opens it on the whole screen; it plays the Stack Player's own copy, so the page goes up with a player that has it
